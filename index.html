FIRST-TIME PATIENT GUIDE

Your First Visit: What to Expect

Every rehabilitation programme at GEP Rehabs follows an assessment-led four-step pathway:

01

Assess

Physical & functional evaluation of movement, strength, and mobility.

02

Explain

Clear explanation of clinical findings and the root factors behind your problem.

03

Plan

Personalised exercise and rehabilitation programme tailored to your goals.

04

Progress

Regular reassessment and gradual exercise progression as you recover.

Your first visit begins with a detailed physical and functional assessment. Rather than focusing solely on pain symptoms, we evaluate your movement patterns, joint mobility, muscle strength, posture, and functional limitations. We then explain our findings clearly and outline an individualised exercise rehabilitation plan tailored to your recovery.

No, prior imaging is not required before your initial assessment. In many cases, a thorough physical examination can identify the factors behind your problem without immediately needing an X-ray or MRI. If diagnostic imaging is clinically indicated, we will advise you accordingly. If you already have existing reports or scans, you are welcome to bring them.

Clinic visits are ideal for in-person physical assessments, equipment-supported rehabilitation, and guided hands-on exercise progression at our Yanam, Gollapalem, or Amalapuram centers. Online rehabilitation is designed for suitable conditions where guided exercise, movement correction, and progress tracking can be effectively delivered through video consultations from home.

Yes, home visits are available as an appointment option for patients whose mobility limitations or clinical conditions make traveling to a clinic difficult. You can select “Home Visit” when booking an appointment to arrange in-home rehabilitation with our clinical team.

Yes. GEP Rehabs provides structured online rehabilitation for suitable conditions. The process follows four clear stages: an initial video assessment, a personalised exercise programme, guided remote exercise sessions, and regular reassessments to monitor your progress.

We provide assessment-led rehabilitation across seven key clinical areas: Orthopaedic & Musculoskeletal, Neurological, Sports Injury, Cardiac, Women’s Health & Gynaecological, Geriatric (older adult mobility), and Paediatric (childhood movement & development) rehabilitation.

You can choose the branch most convenient to you: Yanam (High School Road), Gollapalem (Main Road), or Amalapuram. If you live outside these areas or prefer remote care, our online rehabilitation service is available regardless of your location.
